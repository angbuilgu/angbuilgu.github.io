STARSLIDE

Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: September 13, 2026

Angbuilgu (the “publisher”) provides Starslide (the “app”). This policy explains information handling in the app, support correspondence, and this website. Privacy contact: sksmsdlscks@gmail.com

1. Game data and device storage

No account or login is required. Progress, stars, hint balance, sound and haptic settings, ad counts, and game diagnostic summaries are stored on your device. The publisher does not automatically receive this data or store it on a game server. You may choose to copy a diagnostic summary and include it in a support request.

App data remains on your device until deleted. You can delete local data by clearing the app’s storage in Android settings or uninstalling it. Depending on your device and operating-system settings, app data may be backed up or restored. Backups are managed by your backup provider, not the publisher’s servers; use that provider’s settings to manage deletion and restoration.

2. Advertising and Google services

In version 0.2.6 and later, all users receive the same child-protective ad settings and may receive optional rewarded ads and interstitial ads between completed levels through Google Mobile Ads. There are no banner ads. Watching ads is not required to progress or unlock sectors.

For advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention, the ad service may collect and share IP addresses and approximate location inferred from them, app/ad interactions, performance diagnostics, and service identifiers such as App Set ID. The app removes the Android advertising ID permission and configures Google's ad SDK not to transmit advertising identifiers, not to serve personalized or remarketing ads, and to restrict content to a G rating. Retention and deletion of ad data follow Google’s policies and your settings.

Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy)

Google ad settings (https://myadcenter.google.com/)

3. Ad consent and choices

The app also sends an under-age-of-consent protection setting to Google's UMP for all users. Under this setting, ordinary personalized-ad consent is not requested. No ads are shown if the SDK does not permit requests or an ad cannot load. If privacy choices are required, “Privacy choices” is available in the app's settings.

4. Children and uniform protection

The app is intended for children and adults and does not ask for age or date of birth. Version 0.2.6 and later also does not request Google Play age information, infer your actual age, or classify you as an adult. Instead, all ad requests use child-protective settings regardless of age.

Hints use a limited balance, and watching an ad is optional. Initial hints, qualifying clear rewards, and free skips remain available without ads. Ad unavailability does not block normal gameplay. Parents or guardians can contact the support email about a child's privacy or request deletion.

Version 0.2.5 checked Google Play age-range sharing information on-device, allowed ads only after an adult classification, and otherwise offered free hints without ads. Age information was not sent to the publisher or stored by the app.

5. Support and deletion requests

If you email us, the publisher uses your sender address, message, and any diagnostics or screenshots you attach to respond and resolve issues. Email is handled through Google’s Gmail service. Correspondence is retained while needed to handle the request; unnecessary information is deleted after it is resolved, except where retention is legally required.

Contact the support email to request access, correction, deletion, or exercise other privacy rights concerning correspondence held by the publisher. Manage local game data and backups as described in section 1. Manage ad data handled by Google through the links in section 2 and that service’s privacy settings. Please do not send unnecessary identity documents or sensitive information.

6. Security and service providers

The publisher limits access to support information to what is needed to handle requests. This website uses HTTPS. Google states that ad SDK data is encrypted in transit using TLS. No method of transmission or storage can guarantee absolute security.

Google (ads, consent, and email) and GitHub (web hosting) process information needed for their services under their respective policies, potentially on servers outside your country.

7. This website

This website has no publisher-installed advertising or analytics scripts or tracking cookies. GitHub Pages, the hosting provider, logs and stores visitors’ IP addresses for security. See the GitHub Privacy Statement (https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement) for details.

8. Changes and contact

We will update this page and its date when information handling changes, and provide additional notice where required by applicable law.
